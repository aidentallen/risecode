# RiseCode 0.27.0 — Glass

Glass adds translucent, tinted or clear player/equipment surfaces with frosted scene
refraction, view-dependent highlights, pixel edges, bloom and animated shimmer. The
module starts disabled, and Other players has its own default-off master switch.

Right-click the Glass Render card and choose **Customize / Preview**. The themed editor
has collapsible target and material sections, normal color pickers/numeric fields,
Crystal/Clear/Frosted/Violet presets, retained body/armor/item overrides and an isolated
rotatable/zoomable player preview. It uses your skin and current equipment, with a
client-safe sample loadout outside a world. See [the Glass guide](../glass.md).

Costs are warmed diagnostics from the same first-person sword/shield fixture at
1600×900 on this shared host, with two alternating Off/Glass rounds. CPU, GPU and memory
are separate measurements. GPU timestamps use the device timestamp period. The GPU
column includes the rendered frame, rather than isolating Glass; CPU measures the render
thread. Frame rate was capped at 90, so wall-frame time is not a throughput benchmark.
Texture/buffer ownership excludes driver overhead. These samples do not establish
multiplayer performance, long-session stability or a general FPS improvement. The raw
`027-costs.json` files also retain per-round samples and CPU allocation measurements.

The native checks and inspected screenshots cover:

- Shared/override inheritance, retained disabled overrides, preset/Custom/reset behavior,
  normal setting serialization and saved-profile round trips.
- Disabled-module sample preview, live equipped preview, rotation/zoom, closure cleanup,
  and no mutation of the live player, equipment or camera.
- First- and third-person native geometry, both held items, independent arms/items,
  mixed native/Glass parts and body/armor materials, and slim/wide default skins.
- Sword, shield, bow/use, trident, filled map data, transparent glass-pane textures,
  enchanted/plain gear, retained/suppressed glint, armor trims and native elytra.
- Swing, crouch/water scenes, camera changes, ViewModel, EnchantEffect and PlayerESP.
- **GPU normal/coverage readback:** a selected player behind an opaque wall submitted
  Glass geometry but produced zero covered pixels; moving the same player in front of
  the wall produced visible coverage. Iris repeats this after shader activation.
- 75%/100% world-resolution scaling, resizing, resource/shader reload, repeated toggles,
  native shadows, injected pipeline-readiness failure, native fallback and retry.
- Disabled gameplay has no Glass draws; disable and preview closure release owned
  resources. Deferred world/hand/preview resources remain separate.

Screenshots were visually inspected on OpenGL, Iris shaders and native Vulkan. Results
include local disposable client worlds, rather than live multiplayer sessions. Water
and animation scenes are smoke checks, not exhaustive animation correctness proofs.
Refraction/frosting are screen-space effects; native translucent sorting retains its
usual intersecting-geometry limitations. Arbitrary shaders, resource packs and external
model/hand renderer replacements have not all been validated. Unknown Iris versions
retain native surfaces with a concise status.
