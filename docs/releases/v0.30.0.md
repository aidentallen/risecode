# 0.30.0 — Liquid Glass, native combat, tactile sounds and privacy modules

Minecraft 26.2 · Fabric Loader 0.19.5 · Java 25

This release merges every 0.30.0 review build and lands the four feature
branches on `main`:

- **Liquid Glass interface** (from 0.30.0-beta.1): shared glass material with
  curved-edge refraction, local luminosity adaptation, directional highlights
  and pooled backdrop blur; floating sidebar, animated selection pill, capsule
  switches, pill slider handles, focused text fields, matching menus, popups,
  tooltips and HUD surfaces. Independent refraction/highlight settings and a
  Classic fallback; full palette/font preset with Undo. Existing saved
  settings remain intact.
  [Material guide](../liquid-glass.md)
- **Meteor-inspired native combat controls** (from 0.30.0-beta.2): the
  KillAura rewrite as original native combat controls with target selection,
  attack timing and rotation handling on the gameplay path. Details in
  [KILLAURA.md](https://github.com/aidentallen/risecode/blob/main/release/0.30.0/KILLAURA.md)
  and the [combat guide](../killaura.md).
- **Tactile click sounds** (from 0.30.0-beta.3): eight original synthesized
  interface sounds — Thock, Creamy, Marble, Bubble, Snap, Ceramic, Rubber and
  Chime — in **Client settings → Sounds → Click Sound** with Preview Sound,
  Click Volume, Click Pitch and the master Sounds switch. No downloaded audio
  or third-party samples.
- **Fly and Hotbar privacy modules** (ported from donut-client, MIT): Fly
  grants vanilla flight with local server authority on hosted worlds,
  restores abilities on disable and disconnect, and keeps temporary flight
  out of player save data through a save hook. Hotbar privacy blurs the
  drawn hotbar, selection border, offhand slot and selected item name after
  the GUI pass and can render held items empty in first person, F5 and
  inventory previews; inventory and use packets keep the real items.

## Verification

- Dependency checksum verification followed by `check build` passed on the
  merged release source.
- Focused packaged-client suites on the production JAR (vanilla OpenGL):
  **19** UI-sound assertions, **12** Liquid Glass assertions, **39** combat
  assertions and the Fly (8 assertions) and Hotbar privacy (7 assertions)
  suites, covering module lifecycle, hosted-world flight authority, save-data
  stripping, per-frame hotbar blur, sound registration/playback, glass
  compositing and native combat input.
- The three 0.30.0-beta review builds separately verified Vulkan and
  Sodium/Iris rendering on their branches; this final build re-verified the
  merged result on vanilla OpenGL only. The deterministic scanner and sound
  settings suites (33 + 78 assertions) are unchanged from 0.29.0/beta.3.

Reproduce a packaged native run with:

```sh
python3 scripts/fetch-dependencies.py
GRADLE_USER_HOME="$PWD/.tooling/gradle-home" ./gradlew check build --offline --no-daemon
GRADLE_USER_HOME="$PWD/.tooling/gradle-home" ./gradlew runClientGameTest \
  -PuiSoundsOnly -PclientTestDirectory=build/run/release-0300-sounds \
  -I scripts/panorama-packaged-tests.gradle --offline --no-daemon
```

Fly and remote-server flight remain subject to vanilla server authority;
remote servers may reject flight. Hotbar privacy is a local visual filter and
does not withhold items from the server. Installation backs up the previous
matching JAR outside `mods/`, preserves configurations and unrelated mods,
and Minecraft must restart to load this version.
