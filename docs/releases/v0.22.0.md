# 0.22.0 — Hitboxes and shared target highlighting

Enable **Render → Hitboxes** and choose:

- **Only When Looking** (default): show the entity Minecraft selects under your crosshair, using normal reach and terrain occlusion.
- **Always**: show eligible nearby entity boxes without aiming, with a configurable range (32 blocks by default).

Players, mobs and other targetable objects are enabled by default. Armor stands belong
to Other Entities. Invisible entities are excluded unless Include Invisible is enabled.
The local player, camera entity, spectators and removed/dead entities are excluded.
This module draws visual bounds and does not change collision, picking or attack reach.

In Only When Looking mode, the current highlight moves and resizes smoothly from a
block to an entity, between entities, or back to a block. Moving and crouching entities
update their bounds without restarting the entrance animation. Multipart block shapes
travel with the highlight while blending into the entity box. Block fill fades out
when transitioning to an entity, whose hitbox is outline-only.

Looking into empty space immediately hides and resets the highlight. The next target
uses a center-out entrance pop when Pop Animation is enabled. Direct switches only
pop when Pop On Switch is enabled. Always mode pops newly appearing eligible boxes
and follows their movement thereafter.

**Use BlockOverlay Style** and **Use BlockOverlay Animation** are enabled by default.
The former shares color, opacity, width, full/corner style, gap and see-through settings;
the latter shares smooth switching, timing and pop settings. The existing BlockOverlay
Pop Animation setting therefore determines whether inherited entrance pops are enabled.
Disable either inheritance switch to expose individual Hitboxes controls. Overrides
start from the inherited values on first use and are retained across toggles and saves.

Hitboxes works independently when BlockOverlay is disabled, and may still inherit
its saved settings. Global and module-local Reduced Motion are respected. Target and
entrance state is released on disable, mode changes, resource application, world changes
and disconnect. Existing block-placement animation and shader overlay rendering remain
integrated with the shared aimed highlight.

Validation covers vanilla OpenGL, Sodium 0.9.2 / Iris 1.11.4 with Complementary
Unbound r5.9.3 and BSL v10.1.8, and Sodium Vulkan. Client checks exercise real
crosshair selection, block/entity transitions, entrance pops, inherited and individual
styles, moving/crouching players, invisibility, terrain occlusion, normal reach,
resource reload and disconnect cleanup. Existing BlockOverlay pixel and placement
animation checks are retained.
