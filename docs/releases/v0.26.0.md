# risecode 0.26.0 — Living Panorama

[All versions](README.md) · [Changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) · [Project home](../../README.md)

An optional forest-and-lake diorama replaces the title backdrop while preserving its saved selection. Enable **Living Panorama** in Appearance or the start-screen gear. The centered menu stays in place. The leaf/F1 enters Take a breath; F1, Escape or the return leaf restores it. Existing theme files keep their current appearance.

Six environments blend over three seconds: Misty Dawn, Sunlit Day, Golden Hour, Rainfall, Firefly Dusk and Aurora Night. Resource-pack block models, atlas textures, material transparency and tints feed cached meshes. GPU foliage wind, cursor gusts, water ripples, rain disturbances, fireflies, leaves, rare shooting stars and bounded camera movement animate the scene without creating a world or server. Density, quality, refresh, camera, interaction, optional day/night cycle and quiet environmental audio have independent persistent controls. [Usage and architecture](../living-panorama.md).

## Measured scene costs

AMD Radeon RX 9070, 2048×1152 output, Sunlit Day, 65% tree/undergrowth density, 60 Hz scene refresh, after warm-up. CPU is render-thread CPU time for a scene update; reused texture frames are reported separately in the local JSON. GPU timestamps cover scene passes and exclude native UI composition and button blur. Vulkan timestamps are calibrated with the device's 10 ns timestamp period; OpenGL uses 1 ns. Startup, mesh preparation, input-event picking and long-session behavior are outside these steady-state samples. These are shared-host diagnostics, without an FPS or comparative speedup claim.

| Renderer | Native assertions | Balanced CPU p95 (ms) | Balanced GPU p95 (ms) | Allocated bytes/update p95 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Vanilla OpenGL | 85 | 0.129 | 0.259 | 16,336 |
| Sodium OpenGL | 85 | 0.111 | 0.258 | 16,320 |
| Sodium + Iris OpenGL | 86 | 0.131 | 0.436 | 18,096 |
| Native Vulkan + Sodium | 85 | 0.122 | 0.301 | 8,512 |

The measured Balanced costs meet the initial 0.5 ms CPU / 2 ms GPU targets on this machine. Every measured interval had zero mesh uploads and unchanged owned GPU byte/allocation counts. Balanced uses 68 draw calls/update and 42.85 MiB of owned GPU buffers/targets. Startup through completed fade-in ranged from 1.14 to 1.24 seconds.

Vanilla OpenGL quality samples:

| Quality | CPU p95 (ms) | GPU p95 (ms) | Draw calls/update, average | Owned GPU MiB |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Low | 0.133 | 0.157 | 68.0 | 30.42 |
| Balanced | 0.129 | 0.259 | 68.0 | 42.85 |
| Cinematic | 0.165 | 0.347 | 82.3 | 47.35 |

Low uses 75% scene resolution and simplified distant vegetation. Balanced uses native resolution, cached sunlight occlusion and sky reflections. Cinematic adds a half-resolution planar reflection at 15 Hz. The UI remains native in every mode. Maximum density and all atmospheres were exercised visually; the timing table is the stated Sunlit Day density, rather than an exhaustive timing sweep of every combination. Arbitrary resource packs/shader packs, live multiplayer performance and long-session stability are not established by these tests.

Local detailed evidence: `.tooling/panorama/verification.json`, renderer receipts/logs, `source-preservation.json` and `living-panorama-motion.webm`. Reproduce packaged checks with `scripts/panorama-packaged-tests.gradle` as described in the guide. Earlier Vulkan tick values without device-period conversion are archived separately and are not the GPU measurements above.
